User Experience Evaluation Protocol

Authors: Jan Dragutan, Nina Janikovicova

Evaluation Questions & Tasks

Method: Task-based usability + questionnaires
1

Can users efficiently manage their weekly tasks in the app?

Dependent variables

  • Ease of use of the task manager

Testing

  • SEQ (1-Very hard – 7-Very easy)
  • Additional feedback questions

Task

  1. Think about the things you do weekly (or have to do this week).
  2. Log a couple of tasks into our app (at least 3 for today) and view them afterward.
  3. Edit or delete one of the tasks.
  4. Mark today’s tasks as complete.
  5. Check what tasks you have to do on February 4.
2

Could you find use for the breathing feature? How easy was it to use?

Dependent variable

  • Ease of use
  • Usefulness

Testing

  • SEQ (1-Very hard – 7-Very easy)
  • Question regarding help with relaxation (1-Not useful – 5-Very useful )

Task

  1. Try out a breathing exercise.
  2. Share your thoughts after completing it.
3

Do users find the mood tracking intuitive?

Dependent variable

  • Ease of use

Testing

  • SEQ (1-Very hard – 7-Very easy)

Task

  1. Input your mood.
  2. Change it afterward.
  3. View your mood journey.
4

Qualitative evaluation

Task

  1. If you were to leave a note to your future self, how would you do it?
  2. What do you think about this feature?

Test Results

The table below contains the average SEQ scores for each of our tasks, and the final average score across all tasks.

Task Average SEQ Score (1–7)
Mood tracking 5.6
Task logging 6.1
Task editing 7.0
Task completion marking 6.8
Viewing future task 6.5
Breathing exercises 6.1
Final Average Score 6.3

After analyzing the results, we can conclude that users found our app quite easy to use, with an overall average SEQ score of 6.3 out of 7.0 (1-very hard, 7-very easy). In terms of the qualitative evaluation users were pleased by our implementation of the note for tomorrow feature, and found the breathing exercises quite useful, given a usefulness score of 4.0 out of 5.0. However, after giving additional feedback some users reported slight inconsistencies in our app's UI, including some cards being clickable while others having buttons. Another user felt confusion when it came to the play button for the breathing exercises, as it appeared active when it was not. We have also adjusted some icon colors for better accessibility as one tester struggled with poor visibility. We have taken their feedback into consideration and implemented the requested changes which closely matched issues we noted in heuristic evaluation. There was more additional feedback from users, but we quickly realized not every comment from a tester neccessarly means we have to immediatelly implement it, as most of them were a matter of opinion individual to each person, rather then objective matter affecting the functionality of the app. This was cofirmed by a consistently high SUS score, which was on average 91.7.
